Moves idle talent from roles with surplus to adjacent roles that are short, through upskilling. Sized on the forecast horizon. A starting point for ops review, not an automatic decision.

How it maps to the causal loop

  • Client Demand = new-client calls + existing-client requests (R1 and B2 both run through retained clients).
  • Talent Supply = the company’s ready bench (headcount − deployed), with Philippine search interest as the outside market signal.
  • Demand-Supply Mismatch = forecast requests vs ready talent (bench cover).
  • Allocation Accuracy = match success, placements not replaced within 30 days.
  • Talent Utilization = deployed ÷ headcount (R2).
  • Recruitment & Upskilling = the recommended action and reallocation moves (B1).

Method and limits

  • Trends and forecasts use a log-linear fit across the full shared window (Jan 2025 onward), projected forward. No seasonality adjustment. Specialist demand peaked around Apr–May 2026, so the forecast may run high for those roles.
  • Bench cover = ready talent ÷ forecast monthly requests. Below 0.5 is at risk; 0.5 to 1 is watch.
  • Actions: cover < 0.5 → recruit now (market rising) or upskill (market flat); cover < 1 → recruit ahead; cover ≥ 1.2 with falling demand and utilization < 85% → redeploy; match success < 85% → also fix matching.
  • One request is treated as one seat to fill. Searches show interest, not qualified talent.
